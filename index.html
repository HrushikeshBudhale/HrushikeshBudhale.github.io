About

About me

I’m an AI and robotics research engineer working at the intersection of multimodal intelligence, 3D vision, and autonomous systems. Over the years, I’ve built localization and planning systems for drones, monitoring frameworks for self-driving vehicles, reinforcement-learning planners for mobile robots, and reconstruction pipelines for challenging 3D scenes. I’m most interested in turning ambitious research ideas into systems that can perceive, reason, and act reliably in the real world.

In my free time, I’m exploring

Better simulation frameworks

I’m exploring faster, more composable simulation tools that make it easier to build realistic environments and vary scenarios at scale. The goal is to shorten the path from an idea to a reliable experiment—and eventually to a policy that works on a real robot.

Flow Matching & World Action Models

I’m studying how flow-based generative models can learn coherent action sequences and represent multiple plausible futures. I’m especially interested in world action models that connect learned dynamics with planning and closed-loop robotic control.
